If you need something specific, call or message our care team and we will help you choose the right service.

Can I book a trial daycare visit?+

Yes. Trial visits help us understand your pet's temperament, play style, energy level, and comfort before a regular daycare routine.

What should I bring for boarding?+

Please bring food, feeding instructions, medication if needed, vaccination details, leash or harness, and any comfort item that helps your pet settle.

Do you accept cats as well as dogs?+

Yes. We support dogs, cats, and cherished companions where the requested service is suitable for their needs, temperament, and safety.

Do grooming, vet consults, vaccines, and training happen at home?+

These services can be requested at home or at the farm, depending on availability, location, pet needs, and the service selected.

Are vaccinations required for daycare or boarding?+

We may ask for vaccination details before farm-based services. This helps protect your pet and other pets in shared-care spaces.

How do you handle anxious or shy pets?+

We introduce new pets slowly, offer calmer spaces where possible, and match activity to temperament. Some pets may need a trial visit or a custom plan.

Can my pet stay long term?+

Yes, long boarding may be available. We will discuss routine, diet, health needs, check-in updates, and any special care before confirming.

What happens in a medical emergency?+

We will try to contact you or your emergency contact quickly. If urgent care is needed, we may contact a veterinarian and take reasonable steps to protect your pet.

Where are Jolly Pups Paradise services available?+

Daycare and boarding happen at our pet farm. At-home service availability depends on your area, timing, and care team schedule.

How do marketplace orders work?+

Our marketplace is a curated collection of pet-care essentials. For product details, availability, returns, or order support, contact us by email or phone.

How do I reschedule or cancel?+

Contact us as early as possible. Rescheduling, cancellation, and refund options may depend on the service, notice period, and booking status.
