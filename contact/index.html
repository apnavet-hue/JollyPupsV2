A few quick notes about how our most-used services work.

Can I book a trial daycare visit?+

Yes. Trial visits help us understand your pet's temperament, play style, and comfort level before a longer routine.

What should I bring for boarding?+

Please bring food, medication if needed, vaccination details, and any comfort item that helps your pet settle in.

Do grooming, vet consults, vaccines, and training happen at home?+

Yes. Those services can be booked at home or at the farm, and the booking form will ask which location you prefer.

Where are Jolly Pups Paradise services currently available?+

Daycare plus overnight and long boarding happen at our pet farm. At-home availability for grooming, vet care, vaccines, and training depends on your area.
